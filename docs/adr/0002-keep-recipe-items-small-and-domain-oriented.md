# Keep recipe items small and domain-oriented

Recipe items should remain few and domain-oriented, rather than mirroring every possible recipe-search filter as a separate field. We chose to combine related preferences such as taste, cuisine genre, nutrition, and scene into `レシピの方向性`, while keeping high-signal fields such as `調理時間` and `作りやすさ` separate, so the form stays lightweight without losing the user's important recipe request details.
